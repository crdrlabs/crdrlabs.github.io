# Gift Acceptance Policy for Creative Drive Labs

This policy guides the acceptance of gifts to Creative Drive Labs, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Its purpose is to protect the organization and its donors by ensuring gifts are evaluated before acceptance, handled with transparency, and acknowledged in accordance with IRS requirements.

## General Principles

* No gift is considered accepted unless explicitly accepted by an authorized representative.  Unsolicited delivery of physical items does not constitute acceptance.
* The organization reserves the right to decline any gift that does not align with its mission, creates undue burden, or poses legal or financial risk. Declined gifts will be communicated to the donor respectfully.
* Donors are responsible for determining the value of non-cash gifts and obtaining any required appraisals. The organization does not assign valuations.
* Written acknowledgment will be provided in general, and in particular for all gifts of $250 or more, as required by the IRS.

## Accepted Gift Types

### Cash, Checks, and Zelle electronic transfer

Always accepted. Checks should be made payable to Creative Drive Labs.

For Zelle transfers, use the `crdrlabs` Zelle tag or use the QR provided on the
main web site's donation page.

As of 2026, up to $1000 ($2000 for married filers) can be deducted in addition
to the standard deduction without itemizing.  Consult an expert for details.

### Donor Advised Funds (DAF)

Always accepted.  Grants from donor advised funds are welcomed and are currently the preferred method for donating appreciated securities. The organization accepts DAF grants as cash disbursements from any sponsoring organization.

Donors wishing to contribute appreciated stock are encouraged to contribute shares (held more than one year, as required by many DAFs) directly to their DAF rather than selling first. This approach eliminates capital gains tax on the appreciation and may allow donors to batch multiple years of giving into a single DAF contribution while itemizing deductions, while relying on standard deduction thresholds in other years.

#### DAF Grant Tips

* Our EIN is 41-2661027.  Use this in the search forms.
* There are delays involved in account creation, contribution, and granting.
* Tax deductions apply to the year contribution is made (i.e. before December 31).
* Granting is decoupled from contribution, and can be given in smaller pieces to to 501c3 charities in the desired times and amounts.

### Direct Donation of Publicly Traded Securities

Accepted on a case-by-case basis.  It is easier for us to process DAF, but if needed, we will make appropriate arrangements for direct transfer.  Donors wishing to transfer shares directly (rather than via DAF) should contact the Executive Director in advance to arrange transfer instructions, and should allow for possible delays.

The organization's general practice is to liquidate donated securities promptly.

### Equipment and Tangible Property

Tools, electronics, computers, lab equipment, instruments, and similar items are typically welcomed and accepted, at the discretion of the Executive Director. Items must be in usable condition, reasonably safe, not illegal, and relevant to organizational needs. Donors should contact us before arranging delivery.

### Real Estate

Considered on a case-by-case basis with full board approval. Acceptance is contingent on satisfactory title search, environmental review, and independent appraisal. Costs of due diligence are the donor's responsibility unless otherwise negotiated.

### Vehicles

Not currently sought, but considered, with board approval, if consistent with operational needs. IRS Form 1098-C will be provided to donors of vehicles with a claimed value over $500.

### In-Kind Services and Labor

Gratefully accepted but typically not ascribed a monetary value for acknowledgment purposes, consistent with IRS guidelines.

---

## Restricted Gifts

If necessary, gifts restricted to a specific purpose (e.g., "for the music lab only") may be accepted if the restriction is consistent with the organization's mission and is operationally feasible. Restricted gifts require Executive Director approval and must be tracked separately. The organization will contact the donor if a restriction cannot be honored.
