# Creative Drive Labs Onboarding

To get full access to our community, follow these steps:

1. **Read the rules** in #general-rules and accept our Code of Conduct. **__This is mandatory.__**
2. **Review the usage tips** in #general-usage-tips for hints on using this server effectively.
3. **Request vetting** by sending a direct message to director @James Corey
 or showing up at the facility.  We have a policy that all approved participants must be verified real humans.

Once vetted, you'll have access to all community channels, projects, and events!

**Questions?** Feel free to message the director, consult the [web page](https://www.crdrlabs.org/), or use the web-based contact form.
