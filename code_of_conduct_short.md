# Creative Drive Labs - Community Rules

Welcome! Please read and follow these rules to help us maintain a respectful, safe, and creative community.

## Core Principles

* **Be honest and respectful** in all interactions
* **Be kind and empathetic** - assume good intent and err on the side of forgiveness
* **Welcome everyone** - we're a space for people of all backgrounds and identities
* **No harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior** of any kind

## Privacy & Safety

* **Respect others' privacy** - don't share personal information without consent
* **No security testing** of our systems without explicit written permission
* **Don't record or screenshot** conversations without consent from all participants
* **No stalking** or unwanted contact
* **Online: Don't spam DMs** or overuse notifications - respect others' focus

## Healthy Environment

* **Keep noise down** - treat shared spaces like a library, especially on designated quiet days; use headphones or private rooms for audio/calls (physical facility)
* **Be mindful of focus** - avoid prolonged conversations; respect others' concentration
* **Report safety issues** - let us know about needed repairs, hazards, or concerning behavior

## Prohibited

* No open carrying of firearms (at physical facility)
* No use of alcohol, tobacco, marijuana, or recreational drugs (at physical facility)
* No hate speech or harassment
* No spam or disruptive behavior

## Reporting & Enforcement

* **Report violations** to the executive director at jmc at neniam dot net - all reports handled confidentially
* **Violations may result** in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent removal depending on severity
