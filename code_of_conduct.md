# Code of Conduct

Here at Creative Drive Labs, we are committed to creating a **trustworthy and
respectful** community where everyone feels valued and inspired.

Our space is designed to be a **relaxed and nurturing refuge** for
creative work and learning, welcoming people of all ages and backgrounds.

*This Code of Conduct applies to all Creative Drive Labs spaces—both our
physical facility and our online community (chat, email lists, servers, etc).
Some guidelines are specific to one environment, but the core principles of
respect, safety, and integrity apply everywhere.*

## Respectful Conduct

We expect honesty and integrity in all interactions.

Respect your fellow creators.

Whenever you can, be kind and empathetic.  Err on the side of forgiveness.
Most offenses are unintentional rather than deliberate.

We welcome people of all backgrounds and identities.  Creative Drive Labs is a
space for everyone, regardless of race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation,
age, ability, religion, or socioeconomic status.  We actively work to ensure
all members feel respected, valued, and supported.

Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary behavior will not be tolerated.

While we cannot enumerate every form of harassment, we are committed to
addressing all incidents fairly and thoroughly.

Respect the collaborative and contemplative nature of our space.  Especially
on days designated as quiet days, avoid prolonged loud conversations in shared
areas, and be mindful of others' focus and comfort.  Online, please respect
each others' focus and don't overuse DMs or other notification spam.

## Privacy

Respect the privacy of the other occupants.

* Don't try to obtain others' passwords and sensitive information
* Don't intentionally peek at screens, notes, and other private data
* Assume security testing (penetration testing) our facilities is not welcome
  * Unless we have first given explicit written permission
* Don't take photos or record audio/video of others without consent
  * For minors, parental or guardian consent is required
* Don't leak others' data
* No stalking

## Safety

* Put away tools, chairs, and other equipment
* Clean up spills and messes
* Report needed repairs or maintenance
* Take care with hazardous materials
* Prevent the spread of illness
  * Wear a mask when appropriate
  * Quarantine when sick
  * Wipe down contaminated surfaces
* In the event that current epidemiological data suggests increased risk:
  * If directed by signs, please follow the additional precautions

## Health and environment

* Facility will provide HEPA-filtered air, and hypo-allergenic plants
  * Respect and assist our efforts to curtail allergens and protect lungs
* Keep food odors contained to the kitchen area
* Keep the noise level down--treat it like a library
  * Use headphones for listening, or use a private room for conversations
* Don't shine bright lights at people; let's design lighting with intention

## Prohibited Items and Substances

* No open carrying of firearms on the premises
* No alcohol, tobacco, marijuana, or recreational drug use on the premises
* Prescription medications should be kept secure and private

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct,
please report it to the executive director via email jmc at neniam dot net.
All reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible.

## Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in warnings, temporary
suspension, or permanent removal from Creative Drive Labs spaces, depending on
severity and context.  All reports will be reviewed by the executive director,
and possibly other board members.  Individuals will have an opportunity to
respond before final decisions are made.
